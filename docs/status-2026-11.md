## 4. Model plan

Four tiers, defined in `docs/model-tiers.md`: Nano 46M, Small 111M, Base 598M,
Large 1013M. All are Block AttnRes with a looped middle, top-1 MoE, MQA, 3-bit
KV, ternary experts and NF4 head.

The first shipping target is **Small (111M)**: 18 GPU-hours to train on 2B
tokens, ~163 tok/s on the i5, 36 MB. Base (598M) is the quality tier at 100
GPU-hours and ~49 tok/s. The numbers are calculated from the layout, not
measured; see the caveat in the tiers document.

## 5. Training reality

- `distill` is the only verified training path: teacher weights mapped into the
  student, LoRA and router gradients, checkpoints with resume. It runs on a T4
  through the `.deb`.
- `train` (from scratch) is a stub. The f32 reference model with a hand-written
  backward is the foundation for making it real; it is not wired into the CLI.
- Kaggle gives 2× T4, ~30 GPU-hours/week, 12-hour sessions. Resumable training
  and periodic checkpoint upload are mandatory before any long run.
- Hidden-state distillation at scale is not feasible on the free tier; c-base
  can generate SFT text, not hidden states.

6. **Model**: `evaluate` and `benchmark` before any quality or speed claim.
