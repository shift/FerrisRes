## 1. Model-driven context management

**Claim.** Give the model a *context API* (it decides when and what to compact)
instead of the harness triggering compaction when the window fills. Evaluated in
NVIDIA's open harness across 8K–512K budgets on document research and ML
engineering.

**Numbers.** Higher accuracy, often with fewer input tokens, especially at tight
budgets: GPT 6.1 nearly triples its score at 32K on OfficeQA Pro v2 (21% → 56%);
Kimi uses up to 41% fewer input tokens. Reusing the KV cache of the **unchanged
suffix** after an edit cuts prefill by up to 15% with no measurable accuracy
change. Models differ widely in when they compact, and prompt changes move that
timing.

**What it means for us.** This is validation of the session notebook
(`reasoning-and-retention.md`) as the right shape: a typed context API rather
than a single destructive compaction. Three additions the paper implies:

1. **A drop log.** The community comment is the sharpest criticism: model-driven
   compaction moves the decision into a sampling step, so *recoverability* matters
   more than accuracy. Record why an item was dropped, at which turn, with a
   timestamp. Our notebook already refuses to drop the goal; extend that to a
   reason on every drop.
2. **Count management tokens.** The token accounting must include the calls the
   model spends managing context, or a tight-budget comparison flatters itself.
3. **Suffix KV reuse.** Our runtime caches a prefix; after an edit, the unchanged
   suffix is the larger win. The runtime cache design should treat an edit as an
   invalidation *point*, not a full flush.

**Caveat.** The harness is open source (`nvidia-nemo/labs-OO-Agents`); verify its
licence before borrowing any code. The result is about harness interfacing, not
about model weights, so it transfers to a 111M model more directly than an
architecture result would.

## 2. When reasoning helps a small model

**Claim.** In a coarse-to-fine entity-disambiguation pipeline, reasoning-based
candidate selection gives **negligible in-domain gains** for small LLMs but
becomes **decisive under true zero-shot domain shift**. Two factors separate the
paradigms: reranker uncertainty locates where they disagree, and shift governs
which is right when they do.

**What it means for us.** It is direct evidence for the response-modes gate:
deliberate reasoning must be shown to beat instant *where it matters*, not
everywhere. It also suggests the routing signal for `auto`: **uncertainty +
domain shift**, not task length or a fixed mode. A confident in-domain answer
should stay instant; an uncertain, shifted one escalates. That is a cheaper and
more defensible rule than "think harder on hard-looking tasks".

**Caveat.** One task family (entity disambiguation), and the reasoning paradigm
was trained with distillation + RL. Do not generalise the exact numbers; borrow
the *decision rule* and test it on our eval.

## 3. CARE-OPD: distillation for long-horizon agents

**Claim.** Fully online on-policy distillation is expensive because each update
needs a fresh environment rollout. Prefix replay samples the current student's
response from stored histories, so one trajectory supports many updates. Two
fixes: **student-seeded teacher completions** with response-level confidence
gating (history coverage without unreliable targets), and **hindsight replay
allocation** (use downstream execution evidence to pick consequential turns).

**Numbers.** SWE-bench Verified 59.5% task success, 45% less end-to-end training
time and 44% fewer environment actions than asynchronous online OPD.

**What it means for us.** Our text-aligned KD is offline and teacher-forced; it
teaches *text*, not *agent behaviour*. CARE-OPD is the relevant method once we
train long-horizon tool use: replay from stored histories, gate on teacher
confidence, allocate replay by evidence. The last idea is our own discipline —
"a result is evidence, not a claim" — applied to training data.

**Caveat.** 8B/27B students and teachers, four GPU nodes, OpenHands scaffolds.
Not a Kaggle-quota method. Record it as a later phase after a working small
model and a real agent corpus.
